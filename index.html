Theory Drills

An endless theory quiz: Basics with no notation, plus Trinity-style Grades 1 to 8. Change settings at any time and the next question follows.

Grade content follows the usual Trinity College London theory progression in spirit, but it is an approximation. Check the current syllabus for exact exam requirements. Minor keys use natural minor for scales at Grades 1 and 2, with harmonic and melodic minor added from Grade 3. Counting uses UK terms (crotchet = quarter note).
